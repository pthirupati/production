## Shared storyboard notes (the "money shots")

1. **The fix loop (scene 4)** is the hero moment — record an actual lab session end-to-end: broken service → diagnosis → fix → `Check Solution` → green success. This single clip does most of the selling. Keep cursor movement smooth and deliberate.
2. **The AI interview (scene 5)** — record the interview room with the candidate tile, the AI tile labeled "Browser voice (free)", live transcript scrolling, and the score bars animating in the report. Blur any real PII.
3. **Speed bonus / streak** numbers ("+180 pts · streak ×4") reinforce the gamified, addictive loop — keep them legible.
4. Use the **brand gradient** (cyan→purple→blue) on the wordmark and CTA only; keep the rest restrained so it pops.
5. **Captions always on.** ~85% of social video is watched muted.

## Production guide — how to actually make the video

### Option 1 — Zero budget (fastest): screen-record the included promo
1. Open `marketing/promo-video.html` in Chrome, press **F** for fullscreen (it auto-plays and loops through all 8 scenes).
2. Record the screen:
   - **macOS:** `Cmd+Shift+5` → record selected portion, or QuickTime → New Screen Recording. For a clean 1080p capture, set display to 1920×1080.
   - Or use **OBS Studio** (free) → Display/Window Capture → record at 1920×1080, 30/60fps, MP4.
3. The promo runs ~45s and loops. Stop after one full pass.
4. Add a music bed in **CapCut / DaVinci Resolve (free)** and export. Done.
5. For vertical (Reel/Short), record the same promo but crop to 9:16 in the editor, or re-record with the browser window sized tall.

### Option 2 — Best quality: composite real product footage
1. Record real screen captures of: a lab session (scene 4), an AI interview (scene 5), VMware sim, a coding playground, the leaderboard.
2. Use the promo HTML's title/CTA scenes as intro/outro cards (screen-record just scenes 1–3 and 8).
3. Edit together in DaVinci Resolve / Premiere / CapCut following the Cut A table timings.
4. Add VO: record yourself, or use a TTS tool (ElevenLabs, or your OS voice). The VO column above is the script — read it verbatim.
5. Music: royalty-free from YouTube Audio Library, Uppbeat, or Pixabay. Pick "tech / upbeat / driving" ~120–128 BPM.
6. Export presets:
   - **YouTube:** 1920×1080, H.264, 30/60fps, ~12–20 Mbps.
   - **Instagram Reel / Short / TikTok:** 1080×1920, H.264, 30fps.
   - **LinkedIn:** 1920×1080 or 1080×1080, under 10 min (aim ≤60s for feed).

### Option 3 — AI video tools (talking/B-roll)
Feed the VO script into a tool like Runway, Pika, or Descript for B-roll/voiceover, then overlay the real screen recordings of the product for the hero shots. The product footage must be real — that's the credibility.
